Spotter

Hyperliquid tracker

2026

Spotter is a native macOS menu bar app that keeps your Hyperliquid and Trade.xyz PnL in the corner of your eye — one glance, always current, no chart to overthink. Conviction in the corner of your eye.

Started as a personal project designed to reduce charts staring, make trading more disciplined. It stayed on my Mac before it got a name, hope it earns a spot on yours.

Brahma Accounts

Smart contract wallet, Infra

2022 – 2025

Brahma Account is a programmable smart contract wallet based on Gnosis Safe. Brahma powers apps with programmable capital flows across chains, strategies, and payment systems, linking onchain logic to traditional financial execution.

Retail users access a secure wallet with strategies, swap, and bridge. Protocols use it for treasury management. Developers build onchain/hybrid apps without backend infrastructure.

Swype.fun

DeFi-native spending card

2025

Swype.fun is a DeFi-native spending card that lets you actually use your crypto — either directly or by spending against your on-chain collateral. Integrates with lending protocols (Aave, Euler, Felix, HypurrFi). Marketing positions the product around early crypto ethos and culture.

Brahma.fi, the team behind Swype.fun, has been acquired by Polymarket. Swype Cards now operate under hypurrfi.com.

BuyCrypt

Mobile trading terminal

2021 – 2022

BuyCrypt is a mobile terminal allowing users to connect API keys of exchanges like Binance, Bybit, FTX, Gate and others to track portfolios, trade spot and perp markets, copy-trade strategies, join tournaments, and manage accounts.
